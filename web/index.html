Holds back scams and dangerous attachments before you open them. Free, and it runs inside your own Google account.

  2. 2Paste it into Google

    Open Google Apps Script
    1. Sign in with the Gmail you want to protect.
    2. Press and hold on the code that's there, tap Select all, then Paste. Only the app should be left.
    3. Tap the Save icon. The very last line should say End of EmailSpamdetection; if it doesn't, the paste was cut off, so paste again.

  3. 3Deploy and switch it on

    1. Tap Deploy, then New deployment, the gear icon, Web app and Deploy. Leave "Only myself" as it is.
    2. Tap Authorize access and pick your account. Google warns that it hasn't checked this app, because it's your own private copy: tap Advanced, then Go to Untitled project, then Select all and Continue. Google lists full Gmail access because the app reads and sorts your mail; it never deletes anything without your tap.
    3. Before you tap Done, tap the Web app link Google shows (it ends in /exec). That link is your app. Tap Turn on protection, and the app also emails you its link so you can always find it.

    Tip: on your app page, use your browser's Add to Home screen so it opens like an app.

Stuck? Quick fixes
Can't find your app link
Open script.google.com and tap Untitled project. Tap Deploy, then Manage deployments, then the Web app link (it ends in /exec). If that list is empty, do step 3 again.
The Google page is tiny or hard to tap
Open your browser menu and tick Desktop site, then try again.
"No HTML file named Dashboard" or "Part of the app is missing"
The end of the code was cut off when it was pasted. Copy the app again, paste over everything and Save, and check the last line says "End of EmailSpamdetection". Then tap Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil, Version: New version, Deploy.
"Script function not found: doGet"
The code was pasted inside the old text. In the editor, Select all, Paste again and Save. Then tap Deploy, Manage deployments, the pencil, Version: New version, Deploy.
"Sorry, unable to open the file at this time"
You're signed in to more than one Google account. Open your app link in a private (incognito) tab and sign in with only this Gmail.
The app says Google needs your permission
Tap Give permission and choose Select all. The app needs Gmail access to check your mail.

What it does

Stops risky mail first

Every few minutes it checks new mail for fake senders, look-alike websites and dangerous attachments, and moves those emails out of your inbox.

Shows who emails you

Every website and mailing list you're signed up to, how often you open them, and one-tap unsubscribe or block.

Review before removing

It suggests old mail to clear out: expired promotions, old login codes, big attachments. You decide, and removed mail stays in Trash for 30 days.

Watches your outbox

If your sent mail, drafts or scheduled emails suddenly look like someone else is using your account, it tells you right away.

Why it's safe

  • Your copy runs inside your own Google account, as you. There's no company server, no password to steal and no database.
  • Your app link only opens for your Google account.
  • Nothing is deleted without your tap, and removed mail stays in Trash for 30 days.
  • Switch it off any time under More, or remove its access in your Google account.
  • The code is open: read it on GitHub or look at exactly what you copy below.
