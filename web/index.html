Every few minutes it checks new mail for fake senders, look-alike websites and dangerous attachments, and moves those emails out of your inbox.

A list of every website and mailing list you're signed up to, how often you open them, and a one-tap unsubscribe or block.

Watches your outbox

If your sent mail, drafts or scheduled emails suddenly look like someone else is using your account, it tells you right away.

Why it's safe

Most inbox cleaners ask you to hand a company the keys to your email. This one doesn't: it runs in your own Google account, as you. There is no server that stores your email, no password database to steal and no public page that can reach your mail. Your dashboard is a private page that only your Google account can open.

Set it up (about 5 minutes, on a computer)

  1. Create a new Google Sheet and name it EmailSpamdetection.
  2. In the sheet, open Extensions > Apps Script.
  3. Add the files from the EmailSpamdetection code: Code.gs, Detect.gs and Dashboard.html. Turn on "Show appsscript.json" in Project Settings and paste appsscript.json too.
  4. Choose the setup function and press Run. Google asks you to allow access: this is your own script, so continue.
  5. Press Deploy > New deployment > Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Only myself. Bookmark the link: that's your dashboard.
  6. Optional: turn on 2-Step Verification on your Google account. It protects your email and this dashboard together.

The first scan of your older mail runs in the background and can take an hour on a big mailbox. Protection starts right away.
